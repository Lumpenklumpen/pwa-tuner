## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

First release.

### Added

- Chromatic tuner for guitar (6 and 7 strings), bass (4, 5 and 6 strings) and ukulele.
- 80 tunings: standard and down-tuned, Drop, Double Drop, Open major and minor, DADGAD and sus4, and more.
- Pitch detection with the YIN algorithm, down to the low B of a 5-string bass (31 Hz).
- Automatic string detection, plus manual string selection.
- Semicircle meter (±50 cents) with color-coded status and an "in tune" zone of ±4 cents.
- Favorite tunings, shown at the top of the tuning list.
- Light, Dark and LED themes; the LED theme uses a dot-matrix display.
- English and German, switchable in the menu.
- Adjustable reference pitch A4 (420–460 Hz).
- Installable Progressive Web App: works offline and keeps the screen on while tuning.
